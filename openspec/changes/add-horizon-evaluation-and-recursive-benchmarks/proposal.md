# Proposal

## Why

Daily portfolio attribution cannot establish the quality of a sequential strategy over its intended horizon. The deployed agent also needs a bounded, auditable improvement workflow and real Qwen/Jev benchmark paths rather than treating a static optimizer grid as full recursive research.

## What Changes

- Extend shared evidence contracts with horizon-aware evaluation and immutable recursive-improvement cycles.
- Preserve exact decision-time strategy, holdings, snapshot and objective provenance; distinguish partial observations from mature strategy evaluation.
- Expose a budgeted research operation through same-environment MCP and AgentCore, retaining approval before GPU compute and serving-strategy activation.
- Publish an additive contract package and deploy compatible beta producers and consumers through their existing pipelines.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `horizon-research-evidence-contracts`: Shared contracts for sequential policy evaluation and bounded recursive research lineage.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Existing unarchived implementation changes remain the source of their original requirements.

## Impact

FinancialPlanning owns language-neutral schemas, package publication, fixtures and conformance. FinanceModel owns calculation, benchmark jobs and research orchestration. FinanceLambdasTool owns adapters; FinanceAgent owns investigation skills and hosted orchestration. Beta only; the original paper holdings and selected serving strategy remain subject to their existing approval workflows.
